# Equipment library vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item | An equipment model with a description, such as an 18 V drill. | Catalog |
| Copy | One barcoded physical asset of that model. | Lending |
| Hold | One borrower’s accepted reservation against a copy. | Lending |
| Collection | A staff action that closes an active hold and starts a loan. | Lending |
| Loan | The circulation period from collection to return, with a due date and renewal policy. | Lending |
| Pickup | A staff-facing projection of a hold and preparation work associated with it. | PickupDesk |
| Receipt | A subscriber’s durable record of an incoming message. Receipt and application can occur separately. | PickupDesk |
| Sequence | The revision of an individual hold snapshot; different holds have independent sequences. | Lending publishes it, PickupDesk interprets it |
